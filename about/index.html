Our Process

How a ResinPro project works from start to finish

1. Free Site Survey

We visit your property, assess existing surfaces, measure up and discuss your goals. No charge, no commitment — just honest advice.

2. Fixed Quote & Visualisation

You receive a clear, itemised price and a visual mockup so you can see exactly what the finished surface will look like before a single bit of work begins.

3. Groundwork & Preparation

We excavate to the correct depth, lay a compacted MOT Type 1 sub-base, install edging/containment and ensure proper falls for drainage.

4. Resin Application

Vuba resin and your chosen aggregate are mixed on-site and hand-trowelled to an even 18mm finish. Borders, patterns and multi-blend designs are laid to your specification.

5. Curing & Handover

The surface cures in 6–8 hours (weather dependent). We walk you through aftercare, register your Vuba guarantee and leave the site spotless.

6. Aftercare Support

Got a question six months later? We’re a phone call away. We also offer periodic maintenance visits to keep your surface looking its best.
