Resin Bound Patios

An outdoor space that’s as elegant as it is practical

A resin bound patio gives you a clean, contemporary outdoor surface that’s completely weed-free, slip-resistant and maintenance-light. Whether you’re upgrading tired paving slabs or creating a brand-new entertaining area, resin gives your garden a seamless, premium finish.

No weeds, no moss, no hassle

Unlike block paving or slabs, a resin bound patio has no joints for weeds to grow through. The smooth surface doesn’t trap moisture, so moss doesn’t establish either.

Slip resistant & safe

The textured stone aggregate surface provides natural grip even when wet, making it ideal for families with children or elderly relatives.

Seamless design flexibility

Resin flows around curves, steps and garden features with no visible joints. Add contrasting border colours or create defined zones within your patio area.

Connects driveway to garden

Match your patio blend to your driveway or choose a complementary tone. We help you create a cohesive look that flows from the front of your property to the back.

Permeable & eco-friendly

Rainwater drains naturally through the surface, reducing runoff and meeting SuDS requirements — better for your garden and the environment.

25-year Vuba guarantee

Registered with Vuba on completion, your patio is backed by a 25-year guarantee for total peace of mind.

Common Questions

Resin Patio FAQs

Is resin bound good for patios?

Absolutely. Resin bound gives a smooth, weed-free, slip-resistant surface that’s much easier to maintain than slabs or block paving. It drains naturally so there’s no standing water after rain.

Can resin be laid over existing patio slabs?

In many cases, yes — provided the slabs are stable and level. We assess the condition during your free survey and advise on the best approach for your specific patio.

How much does a resin patio cost?

Resin patios in Cheshire are priced between £150–£170 per m². We confirm the final figure at your free on-site survey so you get a fixed quote with no hidden fees.

Is a resin patio slippery when wet?

No. The textured aggregate surface provides excellent grip, even when wet. Resin bound consistently meets anti-slip standards for domestic use.

How long does a resin patio last?

With a proper base and professional installation, a resin bound patio will look great for 25+ years. Our Vuba products come with a 25-year manufacturer’s guarantee.
