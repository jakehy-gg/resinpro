Resin Bound Pathways

A smooth, safe path that completes your property

Whether it’s a front path from the gate to the door, a garden walkway, or a side access route, a resin bound pathway gives you a clean, level surface that’s safe underfoot in all weather. No loose stones, no trip hazards, no weeds growing through joints.

Slip resistant in all weather

The natural stone texture provides excellent grip even in rain, frost, or morning dew — far safer than polished slabs or loose gravel.

Wheelchair & pushchair friendly

The smooth, bound surface is completely flat with no joints or raised edges, making it fully accessible for wheelchairs, mobility scooters and pushchairs.

Match your driveway or patio

Use the same aggregate blend as your driveway for a seamless look, or pick a complementary colour to define your pathway as a design feature.

Flows around any shape

Curved paths, stepped levels, and tight spaces are no problem. Resin is hand-trowelled on site, meaning we can follow any shape your garden requires.

Weed free & low maintenance

No joints and no loose material means nothing for weeds to grow in. An annual jet wash is all the maintenance you’ll ever need.

25-year Vuba guarantee

Every pathway we install is registered with Vuba and covered by their 25-year guarantee.

Common Questions

Resin Pathway FAQs

How wide should a resin pathway be?

We recommend a minimum of 900mm for a single-person path, or 1200mm+ for a main entrance path. We’ll advise on the ideal width during your free survey based on your garden layout.

Can resin paths curve around garden features?

Yes — one of the biggest advantages of resin bound is that it’s trowelled by hand, so we can follow any shape: curves, circles, or sweeping bends around flower beds and trees.

Is a resin pathway suitable for wheelchairs and pushchairs?

Absolutely. The smooth, jointless finish is ideal for wheels of all kinds. There are no trip hazards from raised slabs or loose gravel, and it meets accessibility standards.

Will weeds grow through a resin pathway?

No. The seamless surface leaves nowhere for weeds to take root. Unlike block paving or gravel, there are no joints or gaps for seeds to settle in.

How much does a resin pathway cost?

Resin pathways in Cheshire are priced between £150–£170 per m². We confirm the final figure at your free on-site survey based on length, width and site conditions.
